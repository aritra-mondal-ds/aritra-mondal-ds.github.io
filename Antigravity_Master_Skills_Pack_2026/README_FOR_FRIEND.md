## How to Install in Your Setup

### For Antigravity Users:
Copy the individual skill folders into your global skills directory:
- **Windows:** `C:\Users\<YourUsername>\.gemini\config\skills\`
- **Mac/Linux:** `~/.gemini/config/skills/`
*(Or place them inside your project root at `.agents/skills/`)*

### For Claude Code / Cursor Users:
- **Claude Code:** `~/.claude/skills/`
- **Cursor / OpenCode:** Place inside your project at `.cursor/skills/` or `.agents/skills/`

Once placed, your AI agent will automatically detect and trigger them on demand!
